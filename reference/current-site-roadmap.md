## Notes on structure

- The roadmap uses **four phases/tiers** as its "status legend": V1 (Available Now), V2 (Available Now), V3 (Enterprise), and Future Vision · Platform. Within V1 and V2, features are further grouped by availability sub-status ("Free tier — all consultants" vs "Unlimited only"; "Bureau tier — live today" vs "Coming to Bureau").
- Feature items are displayed as simple badges/pills (name only) — there are no per-feature descriptions or per-feature status labels in the captured page; status is conveyed at the tier/group level.
- There is no separate "metrics that grow with you" section on this page; the quantitative callouts are the hero stats (4 Phases / 48 Features / 2026 Launch) and the per-tier stat cards captured above.
